Welcome to variant storage.

A variant is a preserved profile — archived straight off the bench. Place one back on a plate, or deploy it to the map to begin coverage.

  • 🧩 Collect archives the entity you are inspecting (costs money and scrutiny) and consumes it — the entity is removed from the plate. A landlord and its tenant are collected together as one pair, and both are consumed.
  • 🔬 Place sets a variant back down on a bench plate, energy and all.
  • 📋 Replicate copies a variant at reduced potency.
  • 🌍 Deploy seeds a country on the world map. The variant degrades.
  • 💰 Sell trades a variant for money — but the responders will take note.
