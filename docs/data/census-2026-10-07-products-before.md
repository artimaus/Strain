## At a glance

- Mean military 64 to 66 from about 50 at seed, the United States past 100 on its 15% floor: the ladder is climbed too fast at the top.
- Mean health 41 to 44 from about 55 at seed: the rich near 90 within a year, the poor decaying for want of energy and materials.
- Short 8 to 12 (Taiwan, South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Algeria, India, the Emirates): the materials now shared with their products.
- World income 52,000 to 56,000 a day, down from 56,000 to 63,000 before the products' upkeep.
- India's technology halves in three years: unpowered, it decays at the economy's unpaid rate.

## Summary

The census that opened the progression round (PRD-08): the end
products on a flat money price per head climbed too fast at the top
and too slowly at the bottom, and their upkeep pushed more nations
short. Famine stayed at 2 (Singapore, the Maldives).
